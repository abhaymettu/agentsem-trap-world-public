# Study 3, after the Study 2 synthetic stress test

27 September 2026. Internal design memo. Not a preregistration or a real-data result.

The program's Study 3 is the Intern Health Study micro-randomized trial, not a continuation of Study 2's passive baseline. The 2018 trial randomized 1,565 interns each week for 26 weeks among mood, activity, sleep and no notifications. It collected daily one-question mood valence, Fitbit steps and sleep. The published paper estimates category-specific effects and effect moderation with weighted, centered least squares; it does not prove that the openly listed cohort-survey archive contains the randomization and daily streams. The archive currently lists only two survey SAS files and a dictionary. Study 3's planned data source is therefore unverified. Do not claim execution-ready access. [Primary paper](https://www.jmir.org/2020/3/e15033); [archive listing](https://www.openicpsr.org/openicpsr/project/129225/version/V2/view).

## Study design, gated

1. **Data and timing gate.** Confirm a release containing 2018 intern ID, week index, assigned notification category, recorded randomization probabilities/availability, actual delivery, daily mood responses, daily steps and sleep, plus missingness flags and prior-week covariates. Confirm linkage and unit before extracting any outcome values. If the openICPSR file is only quarterly survey, seek the MRT data custodian or a verified NDA collection route rather than fitting the wrong data. Access application or contact is separately approved and scoped.
2. **Measurement gate.** From no-notification weeks, test whether mood, steps and sleep support a stable common weekly factor at all. Pre-register orientation, units, transformations and reference scale. A single mood valence question is not a PHQ-9 depression ruler. For each target category, the two non-target streams may anchor a one-factor estimate only under strong exclusion, nonzero stable loadings, and an identified scale. With two non-target streams alone, do not assert that the exclusion is independently tested. Test loadings and factor stability using withheld time/participants and external measures if available; otherwise call the result a cross-outcome pattern, not a latent causal effect.
3. **Randomized contrasts.** Estimate intention-to-treat weekly effects of assigned category versus no-notification by outcome, with participant clustering, calendar week and pre-randomization history. Use actual logged allocation probabilities in weighted/centered analysis; the paper verifies 0.5 daily delivery within a notification-category week but not the four-way weekly allocation probability. Handle delivery/dose as separate secondary analysis, not a replacement for assignment ITT. Direct targeted-channel effect is `effect(targeted outcome) - anchor-implied shared effect` only if measurement gate passes. An effect on steps or sleep can be genuine behavior change and is not automatically measurement contamination.
4. **Missingness and power gate.** Report observed person-weeks and joint coverage of mood/steps/sleep by arm/week. Compare complete cases against predeclared inverse-probability or multiple-imputation sensitivity under defensible missingness assumptions, including a worsening-week MNAR pattern. Simulate weekly sample size/cluster structure and factor loading strength before locking a 0.1 SD bound. If anchor analysis has <0.5 power at the Study 1b tau or data do not support factor identification, report randomized per-outcome effects and no latent contrast. The 300 person-week floor from Study 2 does not transfer here.
5. **Truth-quality gate.** If using quarterly PHQ-9 as external check, model its item information and recall lag; do not score a weekly latent state as if a shrunken PHQ-9 EAP were error-free. In Study 2's synthetic test, the nominal 25% cell gave population S_EAP 30.3% and S_D 36.6% under its reference ruler, while fitted-b decision words differed in 23/40 paired 600-person-week replicates. This is a warning about interpretation, not an observed effect in interns. The weekly mood EMA and quarterly PHQ-9 have different constructs/timescales.

## Falsifiers and decision consequences

- If the three streams cannot support a stable common factor, **no latent claim**. The paper can still estimate randomized effects on mood, steps and sleep separately. That is a design failure for this particular Study 3 estimand, not proof the messages have no effect.
- If an anchor factor is identified but the supposedly non-target streams also show direct category-specific shifts or loading drift, refuse the latent contrast. A positive target effect remains a genuine total effect on that observed outcome. It cannot be decomposed into 'artifact' versus 'real help' from these streams alone.
- If category effects on the targeted channels are small and well bounded after missingness sensitivity, the trap may be small for prompt-sized notifications; no extrapolation to stronger agent actions. If confidence intervals are wide, say inconclusive rather than 'no contamination'.
- Only if scale, exclusions and coverage gates pass should the original PROGRAM-SPEC-v1 decision rule be considered for a dated, pre-data Study 3 spec: targeted improvement and anchor-implied effect separated by a 95% interval. Correct comparison uses the covariance of the two estimates and a simultaneous plan for three categories; nonoverlapping intervals are not an efficient or sufficient definition of their difference.

## Code and near-term work

A synthetic identification check can be written without the dataset: generate 26-week, four-arm assignments and three outcomes under (A) shared latent benefit only, (B) direct targeted-channel benefit only, (C) mixed benefit, (D) non-target spillover, (E) changing loadings, and (F) state-dependent missingness. Fit randomized outcome ITT; attempt anchor decomposition only when parameter count and reference scale identify it. Show which worlds are observationally equivalent. Tests should fail closed when metadata/anchors are missing, leave Study 2's frozen files alone and label all simulated numbers.

Before a Mac commit, obtain the source release or custodian confirmation of the weekly MRT fields and a cold methods review of the identifiability argument. The present memo is a research plan, not a study registration. No participant record was accessed, no access request was filed, and no provider compute was purchased.
